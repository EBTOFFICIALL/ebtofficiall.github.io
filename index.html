Our story

Rooted in Esiama. Inspired by service.

Founded through the shared commitment of Esiama indigenes living abroad, EBT brings people together to improve community life through practical, locally grounded action.

Our beginnings

Following a visit to Esiama in June 2024, founder Benjamin Yeboah Nyarko was inspired by the challenges facing children, schools, healthcare and sanitation. He shared a vision of collective action with fellow Esiama indigenes abroad, who came together to support the initiative.

What guides us

Compassion, unity, responsible stewardship and community action. We connect supporters at home and abroad to help address local priorities and build lasting opportunities.

Purpose & direction

Our mission, vision and priorities

Our work centres on the wellbeing and sustainable development of Esiama and its people.

Mission

Community-led progress

Champion initiatives that improve living conditions through education, healthcare, sanitation, cultural preservation and other development projects.

Vision

Impact beyond today

Build an admired and impactful grassroots organisation in Esiama, with a long-term ambition to inspire positive change across Nzema communities.

Objectives

Opportunity for all

Support youth empowerment, mentorship, sports, local culture and partnerships that strengthen community participation.

Our development priorities

Education & skills

Explore scholarships, educational supplies and youth mentorship opportunities.

Health & sanitation

Build on community clean-up projects and explore partnerships supporting local clinics and health education.

Heritage & community

Promote Esiama's cultural identity, local history and community participation.
