Who we are

Rooted in Esiama. United for progress.

EBT connects people who care about Esiama, including community members at home and abroad, to contribute time, ideas, skills and resources towards meaningful local development.

Get in touch
Our purpose

To encourage practical community action, responsible participation and long-term development that benefits Esiama and its people.

Our vision

A united, thriving and resilient Esiama where everyone has a role in building a better future.
