Who we are

Building lasting change, together.

Esiama Boɛyɛlɛ Ti is a community-focused initiative committed to mobilising people, ideas and resources for meaningful development in Esiama, Western Region, Ghana.

From environmental clean-up activities to future health, education and community support programmes, we believe that real progress begins with collective responsibility.
